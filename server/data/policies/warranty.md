## Coverage period

Products are covered by the warranty for 12 months from the delivery date (shipments.delivered_at). A warranty claim must be filed within that period.

## Covered defects

The warranty covers manufacturing faults and items that are dead on arrival. No other type of defect is covered.

## Exclusions

The warranty does not cover water damage, physical misuse, unauthorized repair or normal wear. Claims caused by any of these are declined.

## Damage on arrival

Damage on arrival must be reported within 30 days of the delivery date (shipments.delivered_at) with at least one photo. The customer chooses a replacement or a full refund. This is handled together with the damaged item rules of the current refund policy.

## Filing a claim

To file a warranty claim, the customer provides the order number, a description of the problem and photos. Decisions are made within 2 business days of receiving the claim.

## Warranty remedies

An approved warranty claim is resolved by a replacement of the same product. A refund is given only when no replacement is available, and it is the amount actually paid for the item (orders.subtotal minus orders.discount_amount, shared across the order lines). A claim made after 12 months from the delivery date (shipments.delivered_at) is declined.

## Replacement shipping

Approved warranty replacements are dispatched within 3 business days of approval. Replacement shipping is at no cost to the customer.
