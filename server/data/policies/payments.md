## Refund destination

Refunds are always sent to the original payment method used for the order (payments.method). They are never sent to a different card, account or payment method.

## Card refunds

Credit and debit card refunds appear on the customer's statement within 5 to 7 business days. The clock starts on the day the refund is issued.

## UPI refunds

UPI refunds appear in the customer's account within 2 to 3 business days. The clock starts on the day the refund is issued.

## PayPal refunds

PayPal refunds appear in the customer's PayPal balance within 3 to 5 business days. The clock starts on the day the refund is issued.

## Partial refunds

When only part of an order is refunded, the payment is marked as partially refunded (payments.status). The discount is applied proportionally to the refunded items, so the refund matches the amount actually paid for them.

## Discount codes

A discount code such as SAVE15 applies to the item subtotal only. A refund returns the amount actually paid and never more. Codes are single use and are not reissued after a refund.

## Pending payments

An order with a pending payment (payments.status is pending) is not charged until the payment is captured. It can be cancelled without a fee while the payment is pending.
