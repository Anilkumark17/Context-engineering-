## Return window

Returns under the 2024 refund policy are accepted for 30 days after delivery. The 30 days are counted from the delivery date (shipments.delivered_at), and the delivery day itself is day 0. An order is eligible when its days since delivery is 30 or fewer.

## Refund amount

The refund amount under the 2024 refund policy is the full refundable amount, which is the item subtotal minus any discount that was applied. No restocking fee is charged. Opened items are accepted for return.

## Damaged or defective items

Items that arrive damaged or defective are refunded in full under the 2024 refund policy. No photo is required to make the claim. The claim must be made within the same 30 day window that starts on the delivery date (shipments.delivered_at).

## Shipping and tax

Refunds of shipping and tax under the 2024 refund policy depend on the reason for the return. The original shipping fee is refunded only when the item is damaged or defective. Tax is refunded proportionally to the amount refunded.

## Non returnable items

Non returnable products cannot be returned under the 2024 refund policy. A product is non returnable when it was marked that way at the time of purchase. The only exception is a product that is defective.

## Refund timing

Refunds under the 2024 refund policy are processed within 2 business days of receiving the returned items. The clock starts on the day the store receives the return, not on the day the customer requests it.
